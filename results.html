Project Results

Tested results

These are the tested results, and they are not figures from the runs on this page. Each ran over seeds fixed in advance and was tested with a paired test, and each carries the caveat beside it. Each line says whether it was pre-registered in git before it ran. The first pre-registration was on 4 October 2026, so the first 30-seed test of the headline and the four-diesel result, run on 2 October, were planned tests and not pre-registered, and the split by assignment is post hoc. Electric trucks count as 0 g CO2-e per km (tank to wheel). The model covers collection routing only, on synthetic requests and a frozen road network. The 1000 g per km diesel factor is not sourced for this truck, so absolute kilograms are not predictions.

  • With one or two battery-electric trucks of four, emissions-aware planning cut tier-1 CO2-e per request by 0.725 kg (28.35 percent) and 0.863 kg (55.78 percent) against distance-only planning, over 100 seeds, pre-registered (the poster prints 28.4 and 55.8). The first test over 30 seeds, run before pre-registration began, gave 0.735 kg (28.57 percent) and 0.837 kg (55.38 percent).
  • Most of that saving, 55.1 to 73.7 percent, comes from giving the longest routes to the electric trucks. Emissions-aware routing adds a smaller, significant saving on top. A post hoc split, a ratio of means.
  • If electric trucks charge at New South Wales grid factors (600 to 780 g per km), the saving falls to 1.48 to 10.75 percent (0.052 to 0.313 kg) per request. Read beside the first line. 30 seeds, pre-registered.
  • With four diesel trucks there was no tier-1 saving. The gradient objective cost 0.66 to 0.92 percent (0.028 to 0.039 kg) more per request over 30 seeds, a planned test run before pre-registration. The pre-registered 100-seed replication gave +0.27 percent, not significant.
  • At published refuse-truck diesel factors of 2,089 to 3,423 g per km, the saving stayed within 2 percentage points of its value at 1000 g per km. Pre-registered.

The scenarios on the Explore scenarios page are a separate set of 90 seeds, shown descriptively. They are not these results and do not replace them. No claim here says emissions are minimal.
