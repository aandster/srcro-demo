Architecture

Drawn with the C4 model, four levels of zoom from the system's surroundings down to one planning day, in Unified Modeling Language (UML) notation: each box names its kind in «guillemets», people are UML actors, a dashed arrow with an open head means "uses", a dashed frame marks a boundary, and level 4 is a UML sequence diagram.

How the Algorithm Works

Requests arrive every business day. Each morning the planner decides which day each waiting request is collected and the route of every truck, then the trucks drive the first day of that plan.
